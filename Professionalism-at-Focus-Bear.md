# Professionalism at Focus Bear

## Research & Learn

### Professional vs. Unprofessional Behaviour
Professionalism in a workplace means being respectful, reliable and responsible while still being yourself.

Professionalism means being respectful, reliable and responsible while still being yourself.

Professional: Clear communication, meeting deadlines, listening to others, accepting feedback and working well with teammates.

Unprofessional: Being rude, discriminatory, ignoring communication, blaming others or creating unnecessary conflict.

### Respectful Communication
In a remote team, I should keep messages clear and polite, avoid assuming negative intentions, ask questions when something is unclear and handle disagreements respectfully.

### Feedback
When giving feedback, I should focus on the work rather than the person. When receiving feedback, I should listen, ask questions and use it to improve.


## Reflection 
- Great teamwork: I have experienced good teamwork in my university when everyone communicated clearly, helped each other and took responsibility for their tasks.

- Clear communication: I will keep my messages clear and respectful, listen to others and ask questions when something is unclear.

- Positive environment: I can be friendly, supportive and open to ideas while also keeping work professional and having fun with the team.

- Preparing for meetings: I will review the meeting topic beforehand, prepare questions or updates, and take notes during the meeting.

- Being proactive: I will take initiative, ask questions when needed, look for ways to improve my work and inform my supervisor early about any problems.

- Following up: I will politely follow up if I need information or action. If there is still no response and the issue is important or blocking my work, I will escalate it to my supervisor or the appropriate person.

- Things I won't do: I won't disrespect others, participate in bullying or discrimination, hide mistakes, ignore important messages, share confidential information or misrepresent my work.
