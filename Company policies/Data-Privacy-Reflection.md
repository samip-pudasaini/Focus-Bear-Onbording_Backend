 Focus Bear collects personal data that’s necessary for specific, clear purposes and handle it fairly and transparently. ### The best practices for Handling Confidential Data are:
 - Following the principlee of least privilege and only accessing information that are necessary for the task.
 - Keep software, devices and dependencies updated.
 - Using secure connections and avoid handling sensitive data over public networks.
 - Storing seensitive data in approved and secure system with multi-factor authentications.

### Responding to a Suspected Data Breach:

 If I suspect that confidential information has been exposed, I should:

 - Stop the activity that may be causing the exposure if it is safe to do so.
 - Avoid deleting or modifying evidence that may be needed for investigation.
 - Immediately report the incident through Focus Bear's approved internal reporting process or to the appropriate manager/team.
 - Clearly explain what happened, what information may have been exposed, when it happened, and who may have received it.
 - Follow instructions from the responsible security or management team.
 - If credentials may have been exposed, change or revoke them through the approved process.
 - Do not attempt to hide the mistake or investigate beyond my authority.
 - Avoid discussing the incident with people who do not need to know.
