# User Manual for Samip Pudasaini

## My role
I am working on onbarding task for backend
The days and times I work are Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday (start: 10-12 to 6-8), during the onboarding tasks, I will be working on irregular times and almost everyday, trying to learn as quickly and as best I can.

## How I Work Best
I do my best work when I have a clear task, enough independence to work through it, and a defined deadline.
I struggle when I am frequently interrupted or when requirements are unclear.

## Communication Style
I prefer messages to be direct, to the point and detailed, without unnecessary repetition.
The best way to reach me is email.
If you need something urgently, contact me through a Discord call or, if accessible, by phone.

## Collaboration & Teamwork
I work best in a team when I have the freedom to work independently without someone constantly checking over my shoulder.
My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is poor communication, especially when people don't communicate delays or problems early
If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means I am listening and there are no substantial changes or updates to the task I am working on. I tend to speak minimally when I don't have anything meaningful to add.

## Focus & Productivity
I stay focused by keeping my attention on the task in front of me.
Things that distract me are unexpected interruptions, especially when someone asks me to check something physically or do additional research while I am already working on a task.
The best way to support my productivity is to give me deadlines slightly before the actual deadline. This helps me manage my time and avoid leaving tasks until the last minute.

## Feedback & Growth
I prefer feedback to be direct and to the point, with a clear explanation of what was expected, what was missed, and what needs to be improved.
When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is to tell me directly and provide specific, constructive criticism so I understand how to improve.
I appreciate it when teammates are being honest and direct about their opinions rather than being vague or unclear.

## Fun Facts & Quirks
One thing people often misunderstand about me is that I can seem either too serious or not serious enough, depending on the situation.
A fun fact about me is that I jump from one hobby to the next like changing a toothbrush, roughly every three months.
My work superpower is that I can hyperfocus when I am interested in something.
