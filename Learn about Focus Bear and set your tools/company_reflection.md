# Company reflection

## Why do you think Focus Bear was created?

Our Founder and CEO, Jeremy Nagel was late-diagnosed with ADHD. He had tried
every app, every system, so he byilt the thing he actually needed, something
that starts the morning and then blocks the rabbit holes before you can fall in,
and forgives me when I slip. Focus Bear is a tool that is build by ADHD brains,
to reduce decisions, removes the willpower tax, amd guides you one step at a
time instead of handing you a blank to-do list.

## What problem is Focus Bear solving?

Focus Bear is solving the difficulty people with ADHD can have with maintaining
focus, managing distractions, and sticking to routines. It provides tools that
help users reduce digital distractions, establish structured routines, and stay
focused on tasks without relying entirely on willpower or motivation.

## Why do you think this mission is important?

‍As someone that has been observing myself and is highly likely to have a ADHD
diagnosis. The mission is really important, as it helps neurodivergent people
stay on track, it reduces the choice paralysis. The hard thing about choice
paralysis is not about getting it not done, its about the dissapointment and
disgust that you feel for yourself that makes it worse. It affects your mental
health and by the time you have actually accepted that you are different from
others and blame yourself for not being "productive" in a world built for
neurotypicals.

Focus Bear's work aligns with my personal values because I am interested in

# Knowing the product

## What did you learn about the product that you didn't know before?

I learnt that you could also trigger Focus Bear activity from an Event in the
calendar. For this you simply follow the following steps:

- Open your Apple Calendar app (or Google Calendar if you are using that).
- Double-click on the event that you want to trigger an activity or focus mode
- Edit the title by adding fbtrigger: followed by the name of the habit or focus mode.
- This will start the activity or focus mode when you click "Attend Meeting" in the event notification pop-up.\
