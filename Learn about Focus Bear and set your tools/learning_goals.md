# Learning Goals

## What do you hope to learn from this internship?

I hope to learn how backend development is done in a real-world team and how an
existing codebase is maintained and developed over time. In particular, I want
to become more comfortable with Focus Bear’s Nest.js backend conventions and
understand how different parts of a production application work together. I also
want to learn how professional development practices differ from university
projects, especially when working with an existing codebase and team standards.

## What skills are you most excited to develop?

I am most excited to develop my backend development skills, particularly with
Nest.js and working with an existing codebase. I also want to improve my
problem-solving and ability to understand unfamiliar code. Alongside technical
skills, I am looking forward to improving my professional written and verbal
communication, especially when giving updates, documenting my work, and
communicating with the team.

## What do you think you can contribute to the team?

I can contribute my existing full-stack development experience and understanding
of backend concepts such as API design, routing, authentication, and data flow.
My previous experience with Express, MongoDB, React, and JWT authentication
gives me a foundation that I can apply to the team's technology stack. I also
try to research tools and technologies carefully before using them and consider
security when making development decisions.

## What is one area you feel less confident in that you’d like to improve?

I feel less confident in professional communication compared with my technical
skills. Working in a team, particularly in a remote or hybrid environment,
requires clear written updates, documentation, and communication about progress
and problems. I would like to become more confident in communicating my work
clearly and asking questions when I need help.

## What does success in this internship look like for you?

Success would mean becoming a more confident backend developer who can
contribute effectively to an existing codebase rather than only working
independently on my own projects. I would like to complete meaningful backend
development tasks using Focus Bear's established conventions, receive feedback
from the team, and understand how I have improved throughout the internship. I
also want to finish the internship with stronger communication skills and a
better understanding of professional software development practices.
