# Use of AI Tools

## Research & Learn 
AI tools such as Claude, GitHub Copilot and AI-first code editors like Cursor are typically used in this role for coding assistance and debugging.

The main benefits are saving time, and helping with repetitive tasks. However, AI carries risks such as data leaks, heavy bias, and being incorrect.

I should never enter any passwords, API keys, confidential company information, private user data or other sensitive information into an AI tool unless it is specifically approved.

AI-generated information should be checked against reliable sources, documentation or my own testing rateher than being accepted automatically.

## Reflection 
I should use AI when it can help me understand a concept or a problem, generate ideas or speed up repetitive work. However, I should still understand the work myself and make the final decisions.

To avoid over-reliance, I will try to solve problems myself first and use AI as a tool for assistance rather than a replacement for my own thinking.

I will protect data privacy by  removing sensitive information from prompts and only using AI tools that are approved for work purposes.
