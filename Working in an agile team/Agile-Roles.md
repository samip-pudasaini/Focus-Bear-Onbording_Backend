# Roles in an Agile Team

## Research & Learn

### Key Agile Team Roles

- Developers: Design, build, test and maintain the product.
- QA: Tests the product to find bugs and ensure it meets requirements.
- Agile Project Manager: Helps coordinate work, remove blockers and keep the
  team aligned with Agile practices.
- UX: Designs the user experience and ensures the product is easy and accessible
  to use.
- Stakeholders: Provide requirements, feedback and business priorities.
- Customer Support: Communicates user issues and feedback back to the team.

### Scrum vs Kanban

In Scrum, roles and responsibilities are usually more structured, with work
organised into sprints and regular ceremonies.

In Kanban, roles are generally more flexible. Team members continuously pull tasks
from the prioritised workflow, collaborate as needed and focus on maintaining a
smooth flow of work.
