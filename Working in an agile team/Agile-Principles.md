# Working in an Agile Team

## Research & Learn

Agile is a way of managing projects that focuses on flexibility, continuous
improvement and delivering work in small increments. Unlike traditional project
management, where most requirements are planned upfront, Agile adapts to change.

The Core Vlaues of Agile are:

- Individuals and interactions over processes and tools.
- Working software over extensive documentation.
- Customer collaboration over contract negotiation.
- Responding to change over following a fixed plan.

### Scrum vs Kanban

Scrum uses fixed-length sprints, usually 1–4 weeks, with structured roles and
ceremonies. Work is planned at the beginning of each sprint.

Kanban uses a continuous workflow where tasks are prioritised and moved through a
board as capacity becomes available. It does not require fixed sprints.

Focus Bear leans towards Kanban because it provides more flexibility and suits a
workflow where priorities can change frequently. It also allows the team to
continuously pick up and complete tasks.

## Reflection
