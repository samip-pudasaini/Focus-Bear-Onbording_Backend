# Agile Ceremonies & Team Collaboration

## Research & Learn

### Daily Stand-ups

The daily stand up is a short, 15-minute (or less) daily meeting to discuss
progress and identify blockers.
It is designed to quickly inform everyone of what's going on across the team.
It's not a detailed status meeting.
There's implicit accountability in reporting what work you completed yesterday in
front of your peers. No one wants to be the person who is constantly doing the
same thing and not making progress.

### Sprint Planning

While practicing scrum, the sprint planning meeting is held at the beginning of
the sprint and is where teams identify what can be delivered in the sprint and how
that work will be achieved.

### Continuous Prioritisation (kanban)

It is the dynamic, ongoing process of re-ranking tasks, projects, or backlog
items based on changing goals, new data, and limited resources.
The core purpose of continuous prioritization is to maximize the delivery of value
while minimizing wasted time and resources in an unpredictable environment. It
moves organizations away from rigid, upfront planning.

### Retrospectives

A sprint retrospective is a meeting to review what was successful during the
sprint and what can be improved upon. Agile teams can specifically review team
dynamics, processes, and tools, then create an action plan.
Retrospective is a time to find out what's working so the team can continue to
focus on those areas, and also find out what they need to improve upon on the next
sprint.

### Backlog refinement

Backlog refinemen is the ongoing process of keeping a product backlog organized,
estimated, and prioritized. Its ultimate purpose is to ensure the team always has
a stream of ready-to-work items.

Agile teams can work asynchronously by using tools such as Slack/Discord, project
boards and shared documents to communicate updates and track tasks. Team members
should document decisions, leave notes.
For different time zones, teams can use overlapping working hours for important
meetings while allowing other work to happen asynchronously.

## Reflection

Agile ceremonies help keep everyone informed about progress, priorities and
blockers. They give the team regular opportunities to discuss problems, clarify
tasks and make sure everyone is working towards the same goal.

I think daily stand-ups are the most important for my role because they provide a
quick way to communicate my progress, raise blockers and understand what the rest
of the team is working on. This helps me stay aligned with the team.
