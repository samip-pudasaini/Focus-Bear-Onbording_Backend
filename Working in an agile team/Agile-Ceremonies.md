# Agile Ceremonies & Team Collaboration

## Research & Learn

 ### Daily Stand-ups
 The daily stand up is a short, 15-minute (or less) daily meeting to discuss progress and identify blockers. 
 It is designed to quickly inform everyone of what's going on across the team. It's not a detailed status meeting.
 There's implicit accountability in reporting what work you completed yesterday in front of your peers. No one wants to be the person who is constantly doing the same thing and not making progress.

 ### Sprint Planning
 While practicing scrum, the sprint planning meeting is held at the beginning of the sprint and is where teams identify what can be delivered in the sprint and how that work will be achieved.

 ### Continuous Prioritisation (kanban)
 It is the dynamic, ongoing process of re-ranking tasks, projects, or backlog items based on changing goals, new data, and limited resources.
 The core purpose of continuous prioritization is to maximize the delivery of value while minimizing wasted time and resources in an unpredictable environment. It moves organizations away from rigidly following outdated plans and steers them toward achieving actual outcomes.

 ### Retrospectives
 A sprint retrospective is a meeting to review what was successful during the sprint and what can be improved upon. Agile teams can specifically review team dynamics, processes, and tools, then create plans to improve the way the team works.
 Retrospective is a time to find out what's working so the team can continue to focus on those areas, and also find out what they need to improve upon on the next sprint.

 ### Backlog refinement
 Backlog refinemen is the ongoing process of keeping a product backlog organized, estimated, and prioritized. Its ultimate purpose is to ensure the team always has a stream of ready-to-work items that align with current business goals, preventing delays and confusion during sprint planning

Agile teams can work asynchronously by using tools such as Slack/Discord, project boards and shared documents to communicate updates and track tasks. Team members should document decisions, leave clear messages and avoid relying on everyone being online at the same time.
For different time zones, teams can use overlapping working hours for important meetings while allowing other work to happen asynchronously.


## Reflection 
Agile ceremonies help keep everyone informed about progress, priorities and blockers. They give the team regular opportunities to discuss problems, clarify tasks and make sure everyone is working towards the same goals.

I think daily stand-ups are the most important for my role because they provide a quick way to communicate my progress, raise blockers and understand what the rest of the team is working on. This is especially useful when working remotely or asynchronously.
