# Agile Workflows & Kanban

## Research & Learn

- A kanban board is an agile project management tool designed to help visualize
  work, limit work-in-progress and maximize efficiency (or flow). It can help both
  agile and DevOps teams establish order in their daily work.

Using a Kanban board helps visualize their workflow, identify bottlenecks, and
improve efficiency by limiting work in progress. It promotes transparency, making
it easy for everyone to seee the status of every task.

- Each column in a Kanban board represents a specific activity that together
  compose a "workflow". Cards flow through the workflow until completion.

- Task flows sequentially form backlog and "TO DO" columns to various active
  stages.
Individual team members handling the tasks are responsible for actively fragging
or moving their coards as work progress.

- When the column is "maxed-out" the team needs to swarm on those cards and move
  them forward before new cards can move into that stage of the workflow. These
  WIP limits are critical for expos ing process problems.
